# Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: full episode plan

44 episodes × 5 minutes ≈ 3.7 hours, in 8 units. ★ = most frequently examined.

Each episode adds one **exhibit** to the Gallery of Ancient India. Exhibits are simplified carved versions drawn for the series. Revered religious figures are never shown as faces (see CLAUDE.md), and the State Emblem (the Sarnath lion capital) is not used.

Status: **to script** · **sample script (approve)** · **scripted** = approved · **done** = video finished.
