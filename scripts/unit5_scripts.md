Episodes 22 to 27. Same format as `sample_ep04.md`.

**Rules applied in this unit.** No boundaries, no outline of India, no kingdom drawn as shaded territory: capitals, ports and findspots are pins on a schematic physical board. Rulers are generic carved figures with nameplates. The Buddha and other revered figures are never drawn as faces or figures — Episode 22's torana and Episode 24's reliquary carry the aniconic rule forward. Where the sources are thin or historians disagree — Satavahana chronology, the Sangam dating, how far Roman trade drained bullion — the Scholars disagree plaque gives each view equal space.
